About

I am an Electrical Engineering student. Most of what I know came from building things outside of class — a discrete logic gearbox with no microcontroller, a hardware-in-the-loop test bench, Apollo 1 (EEG rover hardware and rover software from a 24-hour WeaveHacks 4 build), Axiom (assistive EEG communication concept I pitched at WeaveHacks 4), and a film photography light meter I built because I needed one and could not justify buying one.

I like the part of engineering where something does not work and you have to figure out why. Switch bounce, firmware bottlenecks, noisy sensor data — the debugging is where most of the learning happens for me.

I am looking for a Summer 2027 hardware or embedded systems internship where I can contribute to something real from day one.
