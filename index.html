About

I am a Computer Engineering student transferring to UC Santa Cruz in Fall 2026. Most of what I know came from building things outside of class — a discrete logic gearbox with no microcontroller, a hardware-in-the-loop test bench and Axiom control stack for an RC vehicle I am turning into an F1-style car from scratch, and a film photography light meter I built because I needed one and could not justify buying one.

I like the part of engineering where something does not work and you have to figure out why. Switch bounce, firmware bottlenecks, noisy sensor data — the debugging is where most of the learning happens for me.

I am looking for a Summer 2026 hardware or embedded systems internship where I can contribute to something real from day one.
