2026 ESP32 · Computer Vision · Touchscreen UI · FastAPI/WebSocket · MuJoCo/PPO

Autonomous Swarm Robotics (In Progress)

Working toward a multi-robot swarm, one robot at a time. The first rover now drives itself around obstacles using a camera and a ToF sensor together, pivots and drives straight reliably after tracking down two real motor-stall bugs, recovers on its own from a Bluetooth link going silently dead, and talks to a touchscreen panel live over WiFi. I've also trained a reinforcement-learning policy in a MuJoCo simulation of the real robot. After finding a sensor bug that had it training blind, and a reward-design flaw that let more training make it more reckless, it now avoids obstacles it's never seen before on 99.5% of randomized test layouts. Hasn't touched the real robot yet, and swarm logic is still ahead of all of that.

Custom Drone Flight Controller (In Progress)

A 4-layer STM32F405 flight controller I designed from scratch in KiCad, built for one of the drones joining the swarm robotics project. ICM-42688-P IMU on its own quiet 3.3V rail, DPS368 barometer, analog OSD, blackbox flash, dual buck/LDO power tree with reverse-voltage protection. Schematic and routing are done and gerbers are with PCBWay; boards aren't back yet, so nothing's bench-tested.

Apollo 1 — EEG Brain-Wave Controlled Rover

Built in 24 hours at WeaveHacks 4: I built the 4WD rover and refined its firmware and daemon on a team EEG project. Motors scale with Muse S focus over Bluetooth while Weave and Redis handle the upstream signal path. Proportional transistor drive per wheel; on my end the fights were software handoff to PWM and power, where 9 V cells sagged until a DMM proved we needed LiPo.
