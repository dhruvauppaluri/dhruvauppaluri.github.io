About

I’m an Electrical Engineering student at Las Positas College, planning to transfer to a four-year university after May 2026. I like building and debugging real circuits — from breadboard prototypes to hardware-in-the-loop test benches.

My current work focuses on analog circuit analysis, digital logic, and embedded control: validating ideas first in simulation (LTspice), then on the bench with DMMs and oscilloscopes.

I’m looking for Summer 2026 internships in hardware design, power, test, or PCB-adjacent roles where I can own a small piece of a real system and prove it works.

Steering-Actuator Latency Test Bench

Designed and prototyped an ESP-style subsystem for a 1/10-scale RC vehicle; built a hardware-in-the-loop latency test bench to validate steering-actuator latency. Firmware compares driver intent (joystick) vs. vehicle dynamics (MEMS gyroscope), applies an EMA filter to reduce sensor noise, and verifies control-loop latency < 50 ms using 115.2 kbps serial telemetry.
